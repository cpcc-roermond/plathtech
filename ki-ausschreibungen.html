Tenders

AI in Tender Processes

"Sharp analysis. Watertight documentation. For both sides of the table."

Whether you are issuing or bidding on a tender: we bring AI into the entire tender process – more precise, faster and legally sound.

"Tenders are a time sink – and uncertainty always remains at the end." – Requirements catalogues with hundreds of criteria, solution designs that need interpreting, evaluations that must withstand legal scrutiny. We bring AI into this process – on both sides.

Pain points we solve

  • Vague or overly generic service descriptions
  • Lack of comparability between tenders
  • Time-consuming creation and alignment of documents
  • Difficult evaluation of complex IT/AI/cloud solutions
  • Risk of "buzzword tenders" without real substance
  • Insufficient traceability of decisions

For every side of the table

Our support is tailored to both roles in the tender process.

Issuing as a client

For procuring organisations

  • Creating service descriptions Precise, complete requirements catalogues – no forgotten criteria, no room for interpretation.
  • Developing evaluation criteria Objective, comprehensible scoring matrices that withstand legal scrutiny.
  • Systematically evaluating solution designs Systematic comparison of submitted documents against all requirements. Critical details buried in subclauses and appendices are no longer overlooked.
  • Unmasking buzzwords AI-powered extraction of key content and direct comparison of vendor solutions. Identification of risks, weaknesses or pure marketing language without substance.
  • Securing award decisions Complete documentation of every evaluation decision – including a full audit trail.
Bidding as a provider

For bidding companies

  • Analysing tenders Rapid extraction of core requirements. What is really being asked for? Where are the pitfalls?
  • Ensuring complete documents Automatic review of your service descriptions and solution designs for gaps, contradictions and ambiguities.
  • Clarification & harmonisation Concrete proposals to clarify and harmonise terms and structures consistently across all documents.
  • Efficiency at the push of a button Rapid creation of RFP/RFI drafts from just a few bullet points. Structured proposals that address all criteria – nothing forgotten, nothing duplicated.
  • Preparing contract negotiations Analysis of contract drafts for risks and critical clauses. Preparation of razor-sharp negotiation arguments based on identified weaknesses.

The result: Significantly reduced turnaround times, higher quality and clarity of requirements, better comparability of vendors and better-informed, traceable decisions with drastically reduced risk at contract signing – regardless of which side of the table you sit on.
