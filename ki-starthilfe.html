AI Kickstart

"We know AI matters – but where do we start?"

Everyone is talking about AI, but what does it actually mean for your business? Before licensing expensive tools or launching unfocused projects, you need clarity.

Our potential analysis examines your business in depth and delivers well-founded answers and realistic goals – no false promises.

How the analysis works

Three clearly defined steps – from the initial stocktake to the finished roadmap.

  1. 1

    Focus

    Targeted identification of real pain points where AI and automation can make a decisive difference.

    We look closely – no buzzwords, no sugarcoating.

  2. 2

    The Assessment

    A joint review of your current status quo – which tools are already in use and what AI knowledge exists within your team?

    Analysing your digital infrastructure and your team's capabilities.

  3. 3

    The Outcome

    A clear roadmap showing the concrete impact AI can have on your services and how you can optimise your business model.

    Concrete, realistic, actionable – not just a theory paper.

Key areas of analysis

Three core dimensions we explore together with you.

Needs Assessment

  • Which pain points really exist in your business?
  • Which tools are already being used?
  • Where are unnecessary friction points today?

Strategy

  • Where can AI support processes and people?
  • Where can AI genuinely replace tasks?
  • What is realistic – today and tomorrow?

Enablement

  • What AI knowledge is available in your team?
  • How can your employees use AI productively?
  • Which training measures make sense?

What you get

  • Honest assessment
  • Realistic goals
  • Individual status quo analysis
  • A clear AI roadmap

No off-the-shelf concept – but a tailored analysis that puts your company, your industry and your people at the centre.
