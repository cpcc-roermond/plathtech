AI Business Optimisation

"Not every process needs to be forced into automation – we find the ones where it genuinely pays off."

From use-case identification through GDPR-compliant evaluation to guided implementation – structured, secure and cost-conscious.

AI projects don't fail because of the technology – they fail due to a lack of structure, data protection risks and poorly chosen models. Our 6-step process brings order, security and cost-efficiency to your AI integration.

The 6-step process

From the first analysis through to live operation – no step is skipped.

  1. 1

    Use-Case Generation

    Targeted identification of the use cases that deliver real value in your organisation through AI. We analyse your business areas and uncover where AI measurably accelerates processes, reduces error rates or meaningfully relieves your staff.

    Not every process needs to be forced into automation – we focus on genuine value.

  2. 2

    Data & Privacy Classification

    Evaluation of identified use cases against data protection classes. Using a purpose-built checklist and detailed questionnaire, we assess every process step for GDPR compliance and EU AI Act requirements.

    Security is the foundation of every AI integration.

  3. 3

    Tool & Model Evaluation

    Off-the-shelf or bespoke? We assess whether GDPR-compliant solutions must run in your own environment or whether hosted services are possible – from ChatGPT and Claude to on-premise models such as Llama or the European solution Mistral.

    Targeted testing finds the technologically perfect fit for your use case.

  4. 4

    Cost Evaluation & Right-Sizing

    The running costs of LLMs vary enormously. Simple use cases can be handled by small, highly efficient models that cost up to 90% less than large models – with comparable quality for the specific task.

    We calculate these levers for you and guarantee maximally efficient operation.

  5. 5

    Informed Recommendation

    After completing the evaluation and cost calculation, you receive a crystal-clear, well-founded recommendation. Transparent, comprehensible and tailored to your specific situation.

    No vendor lock-in, no hidden preferences – only the best solution for you.

  6. 6

    Guided Implementation

    We don't leave you alone with the strategy. The chosen solution is seamlessly integrated into your existing system landscape – with active support until it immediately delivers real value.

    From the first line of code to live operation.

  • GDPR & EU AI Act compliant
  • Right-sizing for your budget
  • From analysis to implementation
