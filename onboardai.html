“New team members fully effective from day one.”

This tool instills the entire project knowledge – history, decisions, pain points, and goals – in minutes. No more being thrown in at the deep end or shaky first appearances.

Part of Project Management 3.0

Thrown In at the Deep End

A new colleague joins the project – and, for lack of time, is thrown in at the deep end. The project is running at full speed, the deadlines are tight, and there is no time for a thorough onboarding. So they get the key access credentials, a brief introduction, and the note: just reach out if you have any questions!

The consequences soon become apparent. The new team member is unclear about their exact role. Project status, prior decisions, pain points, concrete goals – all of it comes together only in fragments. During their first appearances they feel uncertain, and stakeholder questions cannot be answered with confidence. The same applies to project hand-offs: without full context, they rarely work the way they should.

How DAVAI OnboardAI works

  1. 1

    Capture project knowledge

    Project description, project plan & milestones, contacts & roles, current challenges, project history, lessons learned and CV & consultant profile: upload them or type them in your own words.

  2. 2

    Create the digital project file

    All inputs become the project file and project report: created automatically, structured and kept up to date.

  3. 3

    Prepare the interview

    Based on the project file: answer stakeholder questions before they are even asked.

  4. 4

    Steer the project

    Follow tasks, keep the status current and never lose sight of anything.
